ApplyPilot Application Assistant 2.0 for Google Chrome on macOS

1. Double-click the ZIP file to unzip it.
2. Open Chrome and visit chrome://extensions.
3. Turn on Developer mode.
4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped ApplyPilot folder.
5. Download your profile JSON from the ApplyPilot dashboard.
6. Open the extension, import the profile, and add the targeted resume.
7. Open an employer application and click Run application assistant.
8. Answer highlighted questions before using Fill the next page.
9. Review the final page and click Submit yourself.

The assistant detects common hiring platforms, fills approved blank fields,
answers routine work-authorization questions from your profile, and attaches
your chosen resume. It does not enter passwords, answer demographic or legal
attestation questions, bypass CAPTCHAs, or click the final Submit button.
